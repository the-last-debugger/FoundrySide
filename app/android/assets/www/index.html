How to read these numbers
Best recorded price. The highest closing quote recorded for this outcome. Not a price you were offered: there are no timestamps, account terms or limits in this data.
Market-implied probability. The average quote converted to probabilities, assuming the book spreads its margin proportionally. An assumption, not a forecast.
Price-implied probability. One divided by the price. It includes the margin, so it overstates every outcome.
Slips from the latest match-day in the bundle that land near your multiplier, with the probability the market implies. Nothing here is a prediction.
Structures matched on the same net profit. A target is your constraint; this shows what each way of reaching it costs in stake, probability and variance.
Recorded closing quotes from a historical corpus. No timestamps, no account terms and no limits, so nothing here shows a price was obtainable when you act. No profitability, expected-value or user-advantage claim is made or implied.
